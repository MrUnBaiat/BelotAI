The game is played by 4 players divided into 2 partnerships (teams).Please strictly adhere to the following rules:
Deck & SetupDeck: The game uses a 32-card deck (cards from 7 to Ace in all four suits).Initial Deal: Each player is dealt exactly 5 cards.Trump Proposal: After the initial deal, the top card of the remaining deck is flipped face-up on the table. The suit of this card is proposed as the Trump suit (Coz).
Bidding Phase (Choosing Trump)Starting with the player next to the dealer, each player can either accept the proposed suit as Trump or say "Pass".Forced Play Exception: If the initial face-up card is a Jack, the next player must play it. The suit of the Jack automatically becomes the Trump suit, and the bidding phase ends immediately.Second Round: If all players say "Pass" to the first proposed suit, a second round of bidding begins. Players can now declare any of the other three remaining suits as the Trump suit.Final Deal: Once a Trump suit is chosen, the Declarer (the player who chose the Trump) and all other players are dealt more cards so that everyone starts the gameplay phase with exactly 8 cards in hand.
Trick-Taking Rules First Lead: The player who chose the Trump suit leads the first trick.Following Rules: Whenever a card is led, players must respect the following hierarchy of actions:Follow Suit: You must play a card of the same suit as the lead card.Ruff: If you do not have a card of the led suit, you must play a Trump card.Discard: If you do not have a card of the led suit AND do not have a Trump card, you may play any other card.Overruff: If a Trump card has been played (either led or ruffed by another player) and you do not have the suit that was led, you must play a higher-ranking Trump card if you have one.Winning the Trick: The trick is won by the highest Trump played. If no Trumps were played, it is won by the highest card of the led suit.Next Lead: The winner of the trick takes the cards and leads the next trick. The hand continues until all cards are played.4. Combinations (Melds): In this version there are no combinations
Scoring & Game Points ("Bile")At the end of the hand, the total points from captured cards and valid combinations are summed up for each team.The Last Trick ("Pasledu"): If the player who wins the very last trick of the hand is NOT the Declarer (did not choose the Trump), their team receives a bonus of 10 points.Conversion to Game Points (Bile): The raw points are converted into Game Points based on a rounding system (e.g., dividing by 10 and rounding).Examples: 10 points = 1 Game Point; 35 points = 3 Game Points; 66 points = 7 Game Points.Zero Tricks Penalty: If a team fails to win a single trick during the hand, they are penalized with -10 Game Points.Winning the Match: Game Points are added to the overall scoreboard. The game continues until a team exceeds 101 Game Points. Overtime: If both teams pass the 101 threshold simultaneously in the same round, the team with more points 'Bile' wins.
